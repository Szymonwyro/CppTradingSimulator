Conceptuals:
    Bids - resting buy orders, sorted by descending price
    Asks - resting sell orders, sorted by ascending price

Order Arrives:
    Either: passive (limit order) - price is worse than current best opposite quote
    Or: aggressive (crosses the spread) - price is at or better than best quote [order fill]

Workflow at each tick:

PriceStep
.
Agents generate orders based on new price(s) via generateOrders()
.
Orders arrive at OrderBook
.
MatchingEngine processes each order, generates Fills
.
Fill sent back to agents via OnFill()
.
PnL, spread, inventory updated
